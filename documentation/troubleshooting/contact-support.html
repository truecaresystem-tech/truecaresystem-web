Contact Support

Privacy, HIPAA, and production safety notice

Troubleshooting may expose workforce information, Provider configuration, PHI, audit data, billing records, payroll information, or technical identifiers. Use minimum-necessary access, redact public evidence, and perform changes only through approved roles and workflows.

Purpose

Create a Provider-scoped support ticket, attach evidence, approve temporary access, and track resolution.

Diagnostic Checklist

Create the ticket

Enter a clear subject, category, affected module, priority, and detailed description.

Unique ticket number

The system automatically assigns a Provider-scoped support ticket number.

Attach evidence

Upload relevant redacted screenshots, documents, logs, and error messages.

Approve temporary access

Provider-authorized users may approve scoped Platform Support access with an expiration time.

Track communication

Use My Tickets to review status, messages, assigned support staff, and updates.

Audit the session

Approved support and impersonation sessions record start/end, Provider, actor, and material actions.

Recommended Resolution Sequence

  1. Reproduce the issue using the same Provider, user role, record, date range, and device context.
  2. Confirm filters, status, account access, Provider scope, effective dates, and related records.
  3. Capture evidence before changing configuration, data, or code.
  4. Apply the smallest authorized correction and avoid unrelated changes.
  5. Retest the original workflow and one related regression workflow.
  6. Document the cause, correction, validation, and any required follow-up.

When to Escalate

Escalate immediately

Contact authorized Provider leadership or Platform Support when the issue involves possible cross-provider data, unauthorized PHI access, suspicious login activity, data loss, billing submission risk, payroll impact, medication safety, or a critical production outage.
