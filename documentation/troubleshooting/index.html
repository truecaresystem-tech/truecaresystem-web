Central troubleshooting guidance for common True Care System access, workflow, data, Mobile, billing, payroll, and support issues.

Privacy, HIPAA, and production safety notice

Troubleshooting may expose workforce information, Provider configuration, PHI, audit data, billing records, payroll information, or technical identifiers. Use minimum-necessary access, redact public evidence, and perform changes only through approved roles and workflows.

Purpose

The Troubleshooting section provides a consistent, security-conscious method for resolving common True Care System issues. The objective is to restore the correct workflow without damaging production data, weakening access controls, bypassing Provider isolation, or creating untracked changes.

Users should begin with the relevant guide below, preserve evidence, and escalate through the Support module when the issue cannot be safely resolved through approved user or Provider administration steps.

Before Troubleshooting

Confirm the correct Provider

Verify the authenticated Provider and user role before reviewing records or changing settings.

Capture the exact symptom

Record the page, action, message, date/time, user, device, browser, and relevant record identifiers.

Protect PHI and credentials

Redact screenshots and never share passwords, full SSNs, full bank accounts, or unnecessary health information.

Avoid broad changes

Do not delete records, rewrite working code, or alter database data until the root cause is verified.
