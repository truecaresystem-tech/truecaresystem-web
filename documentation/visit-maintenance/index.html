HIPAA, privacy, and proprietary workflow notice

Visit Maintenance may display PHI, workforce information, EVV evidence, GPS coordinates, billing data, authorization data, and audit history. Screenshots in this public guide use redacted or demonstration information. Certain validation logic, adjustment rules, Sandata workflows, billing controls, security rules, and internal automation are intentionally summarized and are trained directly for authorized Provider customers.

Purpose

Visit Maintenance is the operational control center used to compare planned schedules with actual service delivery, review EVV evidence, identify exceptions, correct approved billing time, confirm readiness, and prepare visits for downstream billing, payroll, Sandata, reporting, and audit workflows.

The workspace gives authorized office users a single record view of the Individual, DSP, authorization, call history, GPS evidence, merge eligibility, exceptions, billing decisions, Sandata status, claim status, and change history.

Professional Capabilities

Exception-first review

Highlights missing clocks, unit variances, open visits, cancellations, and unreviewed records so office staff can focus on risk.

EVV evidence

Connects schedule data, mobile call records, timestamps, GPS coordinates, service information, and source information in one record.

Billing readiness

Shows whether the visit, authorization, Individual, employee, GPS, Sandata, and claim requirements are ready for the next workflow.
