Support AI

Ticket triage, diagnostic guidance, and provider-approved support assistance.

StatusDraft

Responsible AI, privacy, and provider-isolation notice

AI features are designed as assistive tools for authorized users. They do not replace required human review, professional judgment, Provider policy, or applicable legal and regulatory obligations. Public screenshots and examples must use redacted or demonstration information.

Purpose

Support AI helps Provider and Platform Support teams classify tickets, identify affected modules, summarize symptoms, review logs, and prepare troubleshooting steps while preserving approved support-access boundaries.

Support Functions

Ticket classification

Suggests category, priority, affected module, and likely ownership.

Issue summarization

Converts the ticket conversation and available logs into a concise technical summary.

Root-cause guidance

Suggests probable causes and safe diagnostic checks without making unapproved production changes.

Log analysis

Highlights relevant errors, request IDs, routes, timestamps, and recurring patterns.

Resolution drafting

Prepares a proposed response, test checklist, or implementation note for support review.

Knowledge reuse

Connects repeated issues to approved documentation and prior resolutions.

Approved Access Boundary

Ticket-based authorization

Provider access must follow the approved support ticket and expiration window.

Session logging

Impersonation and support actions should remain traceable.

No secret collection

Passwords, full SSNs, banking credentials, and private keys must never be requested.

Security and HIPAA Expectations

Authorization

Users may access only the records and functions permitted by their assigned role and Provider scope.

Minimum necessary

Only information reasonably required for the approved operational purpose should be processed.

Human review

Authorized personnel remain responsible for validating recommendations and approving final actions.

Auditability

Material AI-assisted activity should remain attributable, reviewable, and connected to the source workflow.
