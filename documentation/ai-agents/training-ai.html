Training AI

Role-based onboarding, learning support, and procedural guidance.

Responsible AI, privacy, and provider-isolation notice

AI features are designed as assistive tools for authorized users. They do not replace required human review, professional judgment, Provider policy, or applicable legal and regulatory obligations. Public screenshots and examples must use redacted or demonstration information.

Purpose

Training AI is designed to help employees learn approved True Care System workflows, Provider procedures, documentation expectations, and role-specific responsibilities.

Training Functions

Role-based learning

Presents guidance appropriate to DSP, supervisor, office, billing, payroll, HR, compliance, or administrative roles.

Interactive explanations

Explains workflows step by step using approved documentation.

Knowledge checks

Generates review questions and practice scenarios from authorized training content.

Onboarding support

Guides new users through account security, navigation, responsibilities, and escalation paths.

Policy assistance

Helps staff locate the applicable policy without replacing formal training or attestation.

Performance support

Provides just-in-time reminders for complex or infrequent workflows.

Training Governance

Approved source material

Training responses should be grounded in current Provider-approved documentation.

No competency certification

AI interaction alone does not prove competency or replace required training records.

Escalation

Users should contact a supervisor or compliance representative when guidance is unclear.

Security and HIPAA Expectations

Authorization

Users may access only the records and functions permitted by their assigned role and Provider scope.

Minimum necessary

Only information reasonably required for the approved operational purpose should be processed.

Human review

Authorized personnel remain responsible for validating recommendations and approving final actions.

Auditability

Material AI-assisted activity should remain attributable, reviewable, and connected to the source workflow.
