Selena AI Assistant

A provider-scoped conversational assistant for authorized operational work.

Document IDAI-DOC-002
Version1.0
StatusDraft
Last Updated07/28/2026
Responsible AI, privacy, and provider-isolation notice

AI features are designed as assistive tools for authorized users. They do not replace required human review, professional judgment, Provider policy, or applicable legal and regulatory obligations. Public screenshots and examples must use redacted or demonstration information.

Purpose

Selena AI Assistant is designed to help authorized users locate information, understand workflows, summarize operational context, and navigate True Care System. Selena must respect provider scope, user role, minimum-necessary access, and human approval requirements.

Core Functions

Context-aware guidance

Explains available menus, records, statuses, and next steps based on the user’s authorized workspace.

Workflow assistance

Helps users understand scheduling, EVV, billing, payroll, compliance, support, and documentation processes.

Record summarization

May summarize authorized records without changing the source record or bypassing required approvals.

Action preparation

Can draft notes, checklists, ticket descriptions, and review summaries for user confirmation.

Navigation support

Directs users to the correct True Care System page instead of exposing hidden or unauthorized data.

Safety Controls

No autonomous final decisions

Selena does not independently approve claims, payroll, incidents, medication actions, or compliance outcomes.

No credential handling

Users must not submit passwords, authentication codes, full banking data, or other secrets.

Provider isolation

Responses must be limited to the authenticated Provider and the user’s assigned permissions.

Audit support

Material AI-assisted actions should remain reviewable by authorized staff.

Security and HIPAA Expectations

Authorization

Users may access only the records and functions permitted by their assigned role and Provider scope.

Minimum necessary

Only information reasonably required for the approved operational purpose should be processed.

Human review

Authorized personnel remain responsible for validating recommendations and approving final actions.

Auditability

Material AI-assisted activity should remain attributable, reviewable, and connected to the source workflow.
