Governance, security, and compliance notice

This playbook supports controlled healthcare-software operations. It does not replace applicable law, Provider policy, contractual requirements, professional judgment, or formal legal and compliance review.

Purpose

The Company Playbook defines how True Care System plans, builds, tests, releases, supports, secures, and continuously improves its healthcare operations platform. It establishes a single enterprise operating model for internal teams, implementation partners, Provider administrators, support personnel, and authorized stakeholders.

The playbook is designed to protect production stability, provider isolation, HIPAA-aligned auditability, data integrity, service continuity, and consistent delivery across Web, API, Mobile, background jobs, integrations, documentation, and future AI capabilities.

Operating Principles

Protect production

Preserve working behavior and use the smallest safe, reversible change.

Provider isolation

Keep every record, workflow, report, and support action within the correct Provider scope.

Human accountability

Authorized personnel remain responsible for approvals, operational decisions, and regulated actions.

Evidence-based delivery

Requirements, changes, tests, incidents, support sessions, and releases must remain reviewable.
