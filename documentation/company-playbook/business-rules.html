Governance, security, and compliance notice

This playbook supports controlled healthcare-software operations. It does not replace applicable law, Provider policy, contractual requirements, professional judgment, or formal legal and compliance review.

Purpose

Approved rules that protect data integrity, provider isolation, compliance, and operational consistency.

Core Standards and Workflow

Provider isolation

Every business record and user action must remain scoped to the authenticated Provider.

Least privilege

Access is granted only to the roles and permissions required for assigned responsibilities.

Source-of-truth integrity

Operational records must be updated through approved workflows rather than bypassed or silently overwritten.

Auditability

Material viewing, creation, updates, deletion, support access, exports, and approvals must remain attributable.

Stability protection

Do not change working production logic unless the change is necessary, reviewed, tested, and reversible.

Data-change control

Database changes follow the approved schema, manually reviewed SQL, generation, validation, and backup process.

Governance and Documentation Expectations

Ownership

Each workflow must identify accountable owners, reviewers, approvers, and escalation contacts.

Evidence

Decisions, test results, approvals, exceptions, and follow-up actions should remain documented.

Security and HIPAA

Provider isolation, least privilege, minimum necessary, secure handling, and audit logging apply throughout.

Continuous improvement

Lessons learned should result in updated procedures, controls, training, tests, and documentation.
