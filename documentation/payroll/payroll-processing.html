Payroll privacy and security notice

Payroll pages may contain employee identity data, compensation rates, tax settings, bank-account metadata, payroll totals, and reporting information. Screenshots in this public guide use redacted or demonstration information. Access must be role-based, logged, and limited to the minimum information required for payroll, HR, finance, compliance, or authorized management duties.

Tax and payroll responsibility

True Care System provides configurable payroll workflows and reporting tools. Providers remain responsible for verifying current federal, state, and local tax rules, wage-and-hour requirements, overtime rules, filing obligations, banking requirements, employee classifications, and payroll outputs with qualified payroll, tax, legal, or accounting professionals.

Purpose

Payroll Processing generates weekly payroll calculations from approved time and service records, then combines regular hours, overtime, training, sick time, holiday time, PTO, mileage, and other approved extras into employee-level payroll totals.

Header Actions

Field or ControlFunction
Generate PayrollCalculates and saves the selected payroll period. Regenerating the same week updates the existing run instead of creating an uncontrolled duplicate.
Export DOCCreates a payroll document for review, approval, and recordkeeping.
PrintOpens a printable payroll view.

Selection and Summary

Field or ControlFunction
Period Start / Period EndSelects the weekly payroll period, currently displayed Sunday through Saturday.
Staff TypeFilters the payroll calculation to all employees or an authorized workforce group.
Estimated PayrollDisplays estimated gross payroll with total regular and overtime hours.
Payroll Processing SummaryShows the selected period, generation time, and the calculation policy notes used by the page.
NotesExplains editable extra categories and document layout expectations.

Employee Payroll Columns

Field or ControlFunction
Employee / SSN / Type / RateIdentifies the employee, protected payroll identity, workforce category, and rate used.
Hours / OT HoursShows regular and overtime hours derived from approved sources and configured rules.
Training / Sick / Holiday / PTOEditable authorized extra-hour categories included in the payroll document.
MileageApproved mileage eligible for reimbursement.
Regular Pay / OT Pay / Extras PaySeparates core pay components for verification.
TotalEmployee gross payroll total for the selected period before configured deductions and taxes.
