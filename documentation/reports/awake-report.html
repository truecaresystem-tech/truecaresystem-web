Purpose

The Awake Report provides an audit view of shifts marked for Awake Monitoring. It allows authorized users to review the scheduled service period, actual visit timing, reminder activity, DSP awake confirmations, event history, and final Passed or Failed status.

The report supports operational follow-up for overnight or awake-required services and provides a printable timeline based on recorded system events.

Report Detail and Timeline

The detail page identifies the Individual, DSP, service, shift date, scheduled period, actual visit period, reminder and confirmation totals, final status, auto-checkout information when available, and the saved Awake Monitoring event timeline.

Timeline entries may identify when monitoring started, when the DSP confirmed awake, and whether the visit ended through manual or automatic checkout. Users should interpret the report together with the underlying visit record and applicable Provider policy.

HIPAA, Privacy, and Proprietary Workflow Notice

Restricted information

This report may contain Protected Health Information, confidential employment information, operational records, signatures, investigation details, or other sensitive data. Access, printing, and export are limited to authorized users with an appropriate role and business purpose.

True Care System does not publish every internal decision rule, escalation method, audit correlation, compliance control, or proprietary operational process in this public guide. Advanced workflow instruction is provided directly to authorized personnel of Providers that are official True Care System customers.

Security and Best Practices

Verify the selected Provider, Individual, staff member, date range, and report record before printing or exporting. Store generated files only in approved locations, do not share sensitive information through unapproved channels, and follow the Provider's retention and destruction requirements.
