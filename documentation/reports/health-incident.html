Health & Incident

Review Health & Incident reports, investigations, evidence, audit history, and case closure.

Purpose

The Health & Incident workspace supports the controlled review of incident reports submitted by DSP staff. Authorized users can review the original report, complete the supervisor's initial review, assign a Certified Investigator, document investigation findings, upload evidence, review the audit trail, and record the final administrative decision.

The workflow is designed to preserve the original submitted information while adding supervisory, investigative, compliance, and closure documentation.

Workspace

Case Review Workflow

  1. Review the original report.
    Confirm the Individual, DSP, report date, incident type, location, description, witnesses, and supporting notes.
  2. Complete Supervisor Initial Review.
    Record the decision, actions taken, and the current case status.
  3. Assign a Certified Investigator when required.
    Save the investigator's name, email, phone, and assignment history.
  4. Document the investigation.
    Enter findings, root cause, witness notes, corrective actions, recommendations, investigator identity, and completion time.
  5. Complete final administrative closure.
    Record the final decision, final summary, and whether the DSP may view the case outcome.

Investigation and Evidence

The investigation area may include evidence reviewed, persons interviewed, injury assessment, notification history, regulatory reporting determination, abuse or neglect screening, law-enforcement involvement, medical treatment, overall risk, case outcome, follow-up dates, supervisor approval, and QA or Compliance review.

Attachments are categorized and uploaded as supporting evidence. The timeline records major case actions such as review saved, attachment uploaded, investigator assigned, investigation submitted, and case closed.

Case Status and Final Decision

REVIEWSupervisor decision

Documents the initial determination and immediate actions taken.

INVESTIGATIONCI findings

Records evidence, root cause, corrective actions, and recommendations.

CLOSUREAdministrator decision

Confirms the case outcome and official close-case summary.

HIPAA, Privacy, and Proprietary Workflow Notice

Restricted information

This report may contain Protected Health Information, confidential employment information, operational records, signatures, investigation details, or other sensitive data. Access, printing, and export are limited to authorized users with an appropriate role and business purpose.

True Care System does not publish every internal decision rule, escalation method, audit correlation, compliance control, or proprietary operational process in this public guide. Advanced workflow instruction is provided directly to authorized personnel of Providers that are official True Care System customers.

Security and Best Practices

Verify the selected Provider, Individual, staff member, date range, and report record before printing or exporting. Store generated files only in approved locations, do not share sensitive information through unapproved channels, and follow the Provider's retention and destruction requirements.
