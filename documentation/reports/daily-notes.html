Purpose

The Daily Notes report displays service documentation submitted by DSP staff through the True Care System Mobile EVV workflow. Authorized office and supervisory users can review the service date, Individual, staff member, service, scheduled time, actual visit time, mileage, cancellation status, and available report outputs.

This workspace is used to locate completed Daily Notes, compare scheduled and actual service information, and open the generated service documentation for operational or compliance review.

Workspace

Search and Filter Controls

DSPStaff filter

Limit results to Daily Notes submitted by a selected DSP or staff member.

INDIVIDUALIndividual filter

Display records associated with the selected Individual.

DATE RANGEFrom and To dates

Restrict the results to the required service period before applying the filter.

Service Note Output

The available DOC and PDF links open the generated Service Note for the selected visit. Depending on the service workflow, the report may contain Provider information, the Individual and staff member, scheduled and actual time, mileage, units, outcome documentation, service location, supports provided, meal information, and required signatures.

Users should verify that the report matches the intended visit and reporting period before printing, downloading, or sharing it through an approved channel.

HIPAA, Privacy, and Proprietary Workflow Notice

Restricted information

This report may contain Protected Health Information, confidential employment information, operational records, signatures, investigation details, or other sensitive data. Access, printing, and export are limited to authorized users with an appropriate role and business purpose.

True Care System does not publish every internal decision rule, escalation method, audit correlation, compliance control, or proprietary operational process in this public guide. Advanced workflow instruction is provided directly to authorized personnel of Providers that are official True Care System customers.

Security and Best Practices

Verify the selected Provider, Individual, staff member, date range, and report record before printing or exporting. Store generated files only in approved locations, do not share sensitive information through unapproved channels, and follow the Provider's retention and destruction requirements.
