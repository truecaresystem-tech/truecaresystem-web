Call Off Requests

Review DSP call-off submissions, PTO information, dates, reasons, and approval status.

Purpose

The Call Off Requests report displays DSP call-off and time-off submissions available to authorized office and supervisory users. The report supports staffing follow-up by showing the DSP, whether PTO was requested, PTO type, start and end dates, the submitted reason, and the current request status.

This page is used to locate pending requests, review prior decisions, and identify requests that may require schedule or coverage follow-up.

Operational Use

A Call Off Request does not by itself confirm that all affected shifts have been reassigned. Office or supervisory staff should review the corresponding schedule, determine whether coverage is required, follow the Provider's approval process, and complete any separate staffing actions required by agency policy.

Reasons may contain private employment or health-related information and should only be viewed by personnel with a legitimate staffing, supervisory, payroll, HR, or compliance purpose.

HIPAA, Privacy, and Proprietary Workflow Notice

Restricted information

This report may contain Protected Health Information, confidential employment information, operational records, signatures, investigation details, or other sensitive data. Access, printing, and export are limited to authorized users with an appropriate role and business purpose.

True Care System does not publish every internal decision rule, escalation method, audit correlation, compliance control, or proprietary operational process in this public guide. Advanced workflow instruction is provided directly to authorized personnel of Providers that are official True Care System customers.

Security and Best Practices

Verify the selected Provider, Individual, staff member, date range, and report record before printing or exporting. Store generated files only in approved locations, do not share sensitive information through unapproved channels, and follow the Provider's retention and destruction requirements.
