Performance governance and proprietary workflow notice

KPI scorecards may combine operational, staffing, billing, compliance, and service-quality information. Screenshots in this public guide use demonstration or masked data. True Care System does not publicly disclose every formula, weighting rule, approval control, data-source mapping, or governance workflow. Advanced configuration and implementation procedures are provided directly to authorized Provider customers during training.

Purpose

Goals & Targets converts strategic priorities into measurable commitments. Each goal is linked to a Balanced Scorecard perspective and includes a target, current value, progress, owner, and status.

Goal Fields

FieldMeaningReview
PerspectiveBalanced Scorecard perspective supported by the goal.Required
GoalStrategic outcome the Provider intends to achieve.Required
TargetMeasurable result or threshold.Required
CurrentMost recent reported result.Review monthly
OwnerDepartment or leader accountable for progress.Required
StatusOn Track, Achieved, Needs Attention, or At Risk.Monitor

Quarterly Roadmap and Governance

The quarterly roadmap summarizes expected maturity or target scores by quarter. The governance checklist verifies that goals are measurable, connected to strategy, assigned to accountable owners, and reviewed when risk appears.
