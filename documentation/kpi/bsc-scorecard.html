Performance governance and proprietary workflow notice

KPI scorecards may combine operational, staffing, billing, compliance, and service-quality information. Screenshots in this public guide use demonstration or masked data. True Care System does not publicly disclose every formula, weighting rule, approval control, data-source mapping, or governance workflow. Advanced configuration and implementation procedures are provided directly to authorized Provider customers during training.

Purpose

The BSC Scorecard summarizes overall Provider performance and organizes KPI results into Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning & Growth perspectives. It also displays strategic objectives, department rankings, and score trends.

Scorecard Components

OVERALL SCORE

Provider performance

Displays the current score, period, Provider, and historical comparison.

FOUR PERSPECTIVES

Balanced measurement

Separates strategic performance into Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning & Growth.

HIGHLIGHTS

Key measures

Shows selected KPI values and associated performance scores.

OBJECTIVES

Strategic progress

Tracks progress toward Provider-level strategic goals.

DEPARTMENT RANKING

Comparative view

Ranks departments using the approved KPI framework.

TREND

Historical movement

Shows how the overall BSC score changes across reporting periods.

Review Guidance

Leadership should review the overall score together with the perspective scores. A strong overall score may still contain an at-risk objective or KPI that requires corrective action.
