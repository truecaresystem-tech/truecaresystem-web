Performance governance and proprietary workflow notice

KPI scorecards may combine operational, staffing, billing, compliance, and service-quality information. Screenshots in this public guide use demonstration or masked data. True Care System does not publicly disclose every formula, weighting rule, approval control, data-source mapping, or governance workflow. Advanced configuration and implementation procedures are provided directly to authorized Provider customers during training.

KPI Management Overview

The KPI menu provides Balanced Scorecard views, strategic planning tools, rankings, trends, and scorecards for the organization, departments, managers, and employees. These workspaces support performance measurement across Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning & Growth perspectives.

Recommended Review Sequence

  1. Review the Strategy Map.
    Confirm that mission, vision, priorities, and Balanced Scorecard perspectives are aligned.
  2. Review Goals & Targets.
    Confirm measurable goals, accountable owners, targets, and status.
  3. Review the BSC Scorecard.
    Assess the Provider-level score and perspective results.
  4. Drill down through scorecards.
    Review Organization, Department, Manager, and Employee KPI results.
  5. Use Rankings and Trends.
    Identify strengths, declining measures, and KPIs that require leadership attention.
