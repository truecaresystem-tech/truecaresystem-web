Privacy, security, and administrative access notice

Administrative pages may display workforce identity, contact information, Provider identifiers, organizational structure, account status, and operational configuration. Screenshots in this guide use redacted or demonstration information. Access should be limited by role, Provider scope, and the minimum access necessary to complete authorized administrative duties.

Admin Management Overview

The Admin module is the Provider-level control center for workforce accounts, role assignments, organizational departments, internal announcements, branch and office structure, password security, and Provider profile configuration. It supplies the administrative foundation used by Scheduling, Payroll, Billing, HIPAA auditing, KPI ownership, reporting, notifications, and Mobile EVV.

Available Workspaces

FunctionPurposeAdministrative control
UsersCreate and maintain Provider user accounts, account status, password resets, and role assignments.Restrict to authorized Provider Admin or HR personnel.
RolesDefine role-based permissions across operational, clinical, financial, and compliance work.Apply least privilege and separation of duties.
Department ManagementCreate departments, assign managers, and organize employees for KPI and approvals.Keep ownership current and deactivate obsolete structures.
AnnouncementsSend role-targeted internal communications.Do not include unnecessary PHI or credentials.
Branches / LocationsMaintain regions, branches, offices, addresses, codes, and status.Use consistent identifiers and verified geographic data.
Change PasswordAllow authenticated users to update their credentials securely.Never share or store passwords in plain text.
Provider ProfileMaintain Provider identity, branding, contacts, identifiers, alerts, and defaults.Limit editing to trusted Provider administrators.

Recommended Administrative Sequence

  1. Complete Provider Profile and report branding.
  2. Create branches, regions, offices, and locations.
  3. Create departments and assign managers.
  4. Create users and assign minimum-necessary roles.
  5. Use announcements for controlled operational communication.
  6. Review HIPAA, Login History, and Audit Logs regularly.
