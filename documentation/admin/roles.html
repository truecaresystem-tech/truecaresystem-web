ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL

Roles

Define and govern workforce access according to job responsibility and minimum-necessary principles.

Privacy, security, and administrative access notice

Administrative pages may display workforce identity, contact information, Provider identifiers, organizational structure, account status, and operational configuration. Screenshots in this guide use redacted or demonstration information. Access should be limited by role, Provider scope, and the minimum access necessary to complete authorized administrative duties.

Purpose

The Roles workspace defines what each workforce category may view or perform. True Care System uses role-based access control to separate clinical, operational, billing, payroll, compliance, technical, and executive responsibilities without relying on shared unrestricted accounts.

Standard Provider Roles

FunctionPurposeAdministrative control
PROVIDER_ADMINProvider-wide administration and configuration.Assign only to trusted leadership.
SUPERVISORSupervision, schedules, reviews, incidents, and approvals.Limit to supervised programs.
OFFICEOffice operations, coordination, and communication.Do not add clinical access without need.
DSPMobile EVV, assigned visits, notes, and incidents.Restrict to assigned service information.
NURSEClinical and medication functions.Apply clinical minimum-necessary access.
BEHAVIORALBSS and behavior-support work.Restrict to authorized cases.
BILLINGBilling readiness, EVV submission, rates, and claim support.Separate from payroll where possible.
PAYROLLPayroll rates, processing, history, tax, and deposit work.Restrict sensitive workforce data.
HREmployee records and workforce administration.Limit PHI access unless required.
COMPLIANCEHIPAA, incidents, audits, and regulatory oversight.Maintain independent review authority.
FACILITYHouse and facility operations.Restrict to assigned facilities.
ITTechnical support and system administration.Avoid routine PHI access.
READ_ONLYView-only access to approved content.Use for limited review or audit needs.

Governance

  • Review role assignments periodically.
  • Remove unnecessary access immediately after transfer or termination.
  • Use named accounts and never shared credentials.
  • Review sensitive role combinations for conflicts of duty.
  • Use HIPAA and Audit Logs to investigate role changes.
