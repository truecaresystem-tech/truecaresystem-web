PROVIDER COMMUNICATIONS

Announcements

Send controlled internal announcements to selected roles or all active employees.

Document ID
ADM-DOC-005
Version
1.0
Status
Draft
Last Updated
07/26/2026
Privacy, security, and administrative access notice

Administrative pages may display workforce identity, contact information, Provider identifiers, organizational structure, account status, and operational configuration. Screenshots in this guide use redacted or demonstration information. Access should be limited by role, Provider scope, and the minimum access necessary to complete authorized administrative duties.

Purpose

Announcements provides a controlled internal communication channel for Provider-wide or role-targeted operational notices. Messages can be directed to Provider Admins, Supervisors, Office, DSP, Nursing, Behavioral, Billing, Payroll, HR, Compliance, Facility, IT, or Read Only users.

Controls

FunctionPurposeAdministrative control
Target RolesChooses the intended workforce audience.Leave blank only for messages appropriate to all active employees.
SubjectProvides the message summary.Use concise, action-oriented wording.
MessageContains the operational notice and instructions.Never include passwords, SSNs, bank data, or unnecessary PHI.
Send AnnouncementDistributes the approved message.Review recipients and content before sending.

Recommended Uses

  • Schedule and office updates.
  • Mobile App release and upgrade notices.
  • Training deadlines and compliance reminders.
  • Emergency instructions that avoid unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Role-specific workflow changes.
