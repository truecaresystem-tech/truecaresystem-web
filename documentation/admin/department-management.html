Department Management

Build departments, assign managers, and support KPI and approval ownership.

Privacy, security, and administrative access notice

Administrative pages may display workforce identity, contact information, Provider identifiers, organizational structure, account status, and operational configuration. Screenshots in this guide use redacted or demonstration information. Access should be limited by role, Provider scope, and the minimum access necessary to complete authorized administrative duties.

Purpose

Department Management establishes the Provider's organizational structure. Departments connect employees to managers, support KPI ownership, clarify approval responsibility, and organize clinical, compliance, finance, HR, IT, executive, and program-development work.

Tabs

Departments

Create, edit, activate, or deactivate departments.

Employee Assignment

Assign workforce members to departments and identify unassigned employees.

Organization Structure

Review reporting relationships and ownership.

Department Fields

FunctionPurposeAdministrative control

ManagerAccountable manager or department owner.Keep current when leadership changes.
ActiveControls availability for assignment.Deactivate rather than deleting history.
